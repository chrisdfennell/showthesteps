What Is a Logarithm? How to Evaluate Logs, Step by Step

A logarithm asks: what power do I raise the base to in order to get this number? log₂ 32 asks "2 to what power is 32?" It is the inverse of an exponent.

Try it with your own problem ↓

The steps

  1. Read logᵦ(x) = y as "b to the power y equals x". Rewrite it as bʸ = x.
  2. Ask: what exponent on the base gives the number? List powers of the base if it helps.
  3. That exponent is the answer.
  4. If the number isn't a neat power, use the change of base formula: logᵦ(x) = log(x) ÷ log(b) on a calculator.

A worked example

Evaluate log₂ 32.

  1. This asks: 2 to what power is 32?
  2. List powers of 2: 2¹ = 2, 2² = 4, 2³ = 8, 2⁴ = 16, 2⁵ = 32.
  3. 2⁵ = 32, so the exponent is 5.

log₂ 32 = 5. Check: 2⁵ = 32. ✓

Why it works

Exponents answer "what do I get when I raise 2 to the 5th power?" Logarithms ask it backward: "what power of 2 gives 32?" They are used for earthquake and sound scales, pH, and for solving for an exponent, such as how long money takes to double.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing up the base and the answer. log₂ 32 = 5, not log₅ 32 = 2.
  • Taking the log of zero or a negative number. It is undefined: no power of a positive base gives 0 or a negative.
  • Splitting a log of a sum. log(a + b) is not log a + log b. The rule is log(ab) = log a + log b.
  • Reading a log as multiplication. log₂ 32 is not log × 32.

Questions people ask

What are the main log rules?

log(ab) = log a + log b; log(a/b) = log a − log b; log(aⁿ) = n · log a.

What is the difference between log and ln?

log usually means base 10 and ln is the natural log, base e (about 2.718).

What is log₇ 50 using change of base?

log 50 ÷ log 7 ≈ 1.699 ÷ 0.845 ≈ 2.01. This makes sense: 7² = 49, close to 50.
