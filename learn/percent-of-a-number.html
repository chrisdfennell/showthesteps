How to Find a Percent of a Number

"Percent" means "per hundred", so 15% means 15 out of every 100, or 0.15. Finding a percent of a number means multiplying the number by that decimal.

Try it with your own problem ↓

The steps

  1. Change the percent to a decimal by dividing by 100, which moves the decimal point two places to the left: 15% = 0.15.
  2. Multiply the decimal by the number. The word "of" means multiply.
  3. Check that it is reasonable: 50% is half, 10% is one-tenth, and 100% is the whole thing.

A worked example

What is 15% of 80?

  1. Change 15% to a decimal: 15 ÷ 100 = 0.15.
  2. Multiply: 0.15 × 80 = 12.
  3. A mental-math way to check: 10% of 80 is 8, and 5% is half of that, 4. Then 8 + 4 = 12.

15% of 80 = 12.

Why it works

The word percent means "per hundred", so 15% of 80 is 15/100 of 80. Dividing 15 by 100 gives 0.15, and multiplying by 0.15 takes 15 hundredths of 80. It is the same calculation written two ways.

Common mistakes

  • Moving the decimal point the wrong way. 15% is 0.15, not 1.5 or 15.
  • Mistaking 5% for 0.5. A one-digit percent has a zero in front: 5% is 0.05.
  • Dividing instead of multiplying. "Of" means times.
  • Mixing up the part and the whole. "18 is what percent of 72" is 18 ÷ 72, not 72 ÷ 18.

Questions people ask

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For "18 is what percent of 72": 18 ÷ 72 = 0.25, which is 25%.

Can a percent be more than 100?

Yes. 150% of 24 is 1.5 × 24 = 36. It means more than the whole.

How do I find 10%, 5% or 1% quickly?

10% is one place left (8 for 80). 5% is half of 10%. 1% is two places left (0.8 for 80).
