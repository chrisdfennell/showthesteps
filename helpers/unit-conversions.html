    About the Measuring Tape

    The Measuring Tape converts units step by step: length, weight and capacity in US customary units, metric units, US to metric and back, time, temperature, and rates such as miles per hour to feet per second, along with area and volume. Each step says which fact links the units and whether to multiply or divide, and shows the units canceling. The metric staircase, the gallon kingdom and a thermometer show why.

    A worked example: 60 mph to feet per second

    1. Write down the facts that link the units: 1 mi = 5,280 ft and 1 h = 3,600 s.
    2. Change the miles to feet: 60 mi/h × 5,280 = 316,800 ft/h.
    3. Change the hours to seconds: 316,800 ft/h ÷ 3,600 = 88 ft/s.
    4. Check it backwards: 88 ft/s comes out to 60 mi/h again.

    Common questions

    How do I know whether to multiply or divide?

    Going from a big unit to a smaller one, you need more of them, so multiply: 2 gallons is 2 × 16 = 32 cups. Going from a small unit to a bigger one, you need fewer, so divide.

    How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

    Subtract 32 first, then multiply by 5/9. For 98.6 °F: 98.6 − 32 = 66.6, and 66.6 × 5/9 = 37 °C.

    How many kilometers are in a mile?

    One mile is exactly 1.609344 kilometers, about 1.6. So 5 miles is about 8.05 km.
