How a skill’s status is worked out

from the problems you checked
–Not started
No problem checked yet on any helper tab that teaches it.
◔Practiced
At least one problem checked, but fewer than 2 of your last 5 right first time.
◑Getting it
At least 2 of your last 5 problems right first time.
●Mastered
At least 5 problems tried, and 4 or 5 of your last 5 right first time.

Right first time means your first Check on that problem was right, with no hint. A problem counts once, however many times you check it, and it counts for every standard its helper tab is matched to, so one problem can move a few skills at once.

Problems from practice sets and the problem of the day count too. Tutor me and flash cards don’t. Because only your last 5 count, a skill can slip back if the newest ones go wrong, and come back with a few right in a row.

The match between the helpers and the standards is unofficial.
